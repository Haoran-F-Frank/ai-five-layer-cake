World leader in AI chip design and frontier models, and the largest consumer of data-center electricity. Natural gas is the backbone of the power system, and most utilities are investor-owned (private).

> ⚠️ First-draft data compiled from public sources; “≈” marks approximations or estimates. Each [n] links to the References at the end — the original source prevails.

## ① Energy — Power Supply

Natural gas is the largest source of US electricity, nuclear supplies about a fifth, and wind and solar are growing fast. Utilities are investor-owned (private), federal (e.g. TVA, Bureau of Reclamation), state or municipal, or cooperatives. <sup>[[1]](#ref-1)</sup>

- **Utility-scale generation (2024)**: ≈ 4,300 TWh <sup>[[1]](#ref-1)[[2]](#ref-2)</sup>
- **Utility-scale power plants**: ≈ 11,000+ (≥ 1 MW) <sup>[[3]](#ref-3)</sup>
- **Power-generation employment**: ≈ 0.9 million <sup>[[4]](#ref-4)</sup>
- **Data-center electricity (2023)**: ≈ 176 TWh, 4.4% of US total; possibly 6.7–12% by 2028 <sup>[[5]](#ref-5)</sup>
